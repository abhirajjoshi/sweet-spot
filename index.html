How sure are we? Sources and disclaimer

Official: fixed-date holidays, and 2026 festival dates from published state holiday lists. Expected (marked ~): 2027 festival dates worked out from the lunar calendar. They can shift by a day until the state notifies its official list, usually in December. Fruit, blooms, events and weather are typical patterns, not guarantees.

Maharashtra holiday dates, fruit seasons, events, comfort scores and crowd levels are from general knowledge and not yet verified.
